SmishShield is engineered to address the specific vulnerabilities of the Indian mobile subscriber base by bridging TRAI Telecom Standards with Multilingual Transformer Natural Language Understanding.

1. TRAI DLT Header Verification

Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) regulates headers. Legitimate banks use [Circle][Provider]-[Brand]. Scammers bypass this via 10-digit mobile SIMs (+91-98...) or overseas VoIP gateways.

2. Indic Script & Phonetic Coverage

Scammers use phonetic Hinglish or native Indic scripts (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali) to evade English regex filters. Our multi-script lexicon now covers all 7 critical Indian fraud vectors across all major scripts.

3. DPDP Act & Edge Inference Model

Designed so SMS content never needs to leave the device. The 28ms latency and DPDP compliance metrics represent edge benchmark targets modeling quantized INT8 MuRIL / IndicBERT running locally on mobile NPUs.

Built for Smart India Hackathon: SmishShield represents an end-to-end framework integrating on-device AI classification, TRAI DLT telecom compliance, APK reverse-inspection, and direct citizen escalation into national cyber defense pipelines (1930 & Chakshu).
